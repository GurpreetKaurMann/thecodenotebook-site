NumPy in One Lab: Arrays, Vectorisation & Broadcasting

Squaring a million numbers takes 124 ms with a Python loop and 1.1 ms with NumPy. Here's why — and the five patterns that replace every loop you write. Lab 1 of the complete Data Science course. We build arrays, time a Python loop against a vectorised call, see why contiguous memory and one dtype make the difference, then work through broadcasting, boolean masks, the axis argument, and the three mistakes that silently break results: views, dtype overflow and appending in a loop.
