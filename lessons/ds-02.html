The Data Science Workflow End to End (and 3 Ways Projects Die)

Most data science projects don't fail on the model. They fail before it, or after it. Here's the whole workflow, and the three places projects quietly die. Lesson 2 of the complete Data Science course: the five-step loop (question → data → model → ship → monitor), how to turn a vague ask into a decision-shaped question, checking data for completeness, timeliness and leakage, why you always build a baseline first, what 'shipping' really means, and how to monitor a model that will go stale.
